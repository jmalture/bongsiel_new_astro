## Overall feeling (from the photos)

1. A red-brick North Frisian inn with white sash windows, a slate roof and an oval enamel sign with a black horse head.
2. Inside, the Gaststube is dominated by Delft-blue tile walls, dove/denim-blue wainscoting with oxblood pin-striping and many black-and-white framed prints.
3. Furniture is honest wood: oak table tops, Windsor and spindle-back chairs, leather-backed corner benches, a tiled bar counter.
4. Light is warm and low: brass and enamel pendant lamps, tea lights, candles, poinsettias in winter.
5. The Saal is bright and plain: light walls, long tables, wooden chairs, horse-head wall lamps, big windows onto open marsh.
6. Outside it is all horizon: green dikes with sheep, reed-fringed ponds, the canal at sunset, huge cloudy skies.
7. Thatched Reetdach guest houses and simple, clean modern rooms (white, grey, light oak, green curtains) complete the stay offer.
8. Food photos are honest phone shots on white or black plates – hearty regional plates (lamb shank, burger, desserts), not styled studio shots.
9. Palette: brick red, Delft/denim blue, warm oak, cream white, dike green, sunset amber; brand accent olive green (#879760) with black.
10. Overall: authentic, cosy, a bit nostalgic, family-run "Traditionsgaststätte" by the sea – not luxury; the photography is mostly smartphone quality, so a pro shoot (exterior day/blue hour, Saal set for an event, plated dishes) would lift the new site.
